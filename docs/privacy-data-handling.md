# Privacy Data Handling

Owner: Zerra Studios Privacy Officer (`contact@zerrastudios.com`)

## Data Inventory

- Contact form: name, email, message, optional business name, and quote budget.
- Scheduling: information a visitor submits directly through Calendly.
- Infrastructure: ordinary request and security logs processed by Cloudflare and content providers.

Zerra does not accept user uploads, create visitor accounts, process website payments, or run behavioural analytics on the marketing site.

## Retention Routine

Review retained contact submissions and related email at least once each quarter. Delete records older than 12 months unless they are required for an active client relationship, a legal obligation, or a documented dispute. Apply an earlier verified deletion when a person makes a valid request.

Record the review date, systems checked, deletion period, and reviewer without copying personal information into the record. Systems currently in scope are the Formspree form settings/archive, the recipient mailbox, and Calendly.

## Requests and Incidents

Route access, correction, deletion, and privacy complaints to the Privacy Officer. Verify the requester before disclosing or changing personal information. Record the request date, decision, completion date, and systems checked.

For a suspected breach, preserve relevant evidence, restrict further access, identify affected data and providers, and notify the Privacy Officer immediately. Assess notification obligations using current guidance from the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.
